Accounting reconciliation, limits and contrary evidence

GAAP operating profit 592.6 + SBC 212.6 + long-term incentive 0.3 + amortization 9.3 + litigation 454.9 = adjusted 1,269.7. Prior 759.7 + 203.5 + 1.3 + 3.3 + 8.1 = 975.9. GAAP IOMA is −30.7%, while adjusted IOMA is 54.0%; do not combine them. The $450m class-action settlement is part of the current litigation adjustment. SBC is a recurring economic compensation cost even though excluded from adjusted profit. Excluding a litigation accrual from profit does not erase its cash liability. The release’s narrative SBC amount and reconciliation reflect slightly different attribution; use the reconciliation consistently.

Base case: consumables and service follow procedure and installed-base expansion; stronger system placements support revenue but are more cyclical. The 291 operating-lease placements versus 231 shift revenue and investment timing, so placement count cannot be multiplied by a list price to forecast sales. Adverse case: slower procedures, tariff expense after refunds cease, unfavorable system mix or a hospital spending slowdown compress incremental conversion. The distributor-to-direct transition in Italy, Spain and Portugal creates portfolio effects; a quantified organic revenue bridge is unavailable here. Reported growth is retained, fully normalized revenue is n/a. Monitor da Vinci procedure growth, consumables per procedure, lease mix, adjusted gross margin excluding refunds, cash capex, receivables and settlement payment. Cash/normalized FY2027 conclusions remain below the requested high-confidence threshold.
