Why earnings grew and what might reverse

Product gross profit $68.067m versus $41.788m improves $26.279m; royalty growth adds $44.888m before SG&A. This reconciles the entire gross-profit movement. The company links licensing opportunity to patented power-delivery technology and says it granted its first VPD license to an AI OEM at Stage-2 unit royalty rates. Those are management claims about adoption and enforcement, not independently verified infringement findings or guaranteed customer wins. Release disclosures do not split the $66.613m into current-unit royalties, past-period payments or milestone recognition. Thus a large share of the profit bridge has unresolved duration. A stress case holding royalty revenue at the prior $21.725m, with current costs unchanged, gives revenue $152.226m and operating income $27.260m: profit still exceeds prior $20.907m, but only $6.353m of growth remains, with 15.2% incremental margin on product growth. Fees could also change, so this is explicitly a one-variable adverse sensitivity, not a forecast.

Backlog $406m versus $153m (+166%) and $380m sequentially supports demand but cancellation terms, conversion timing, concentration and royalties included in backlog are not fully established here. Capacity expansion demonstrates expected demand and creates execution, depreciation and cash-spending risk. Product gross-margin gains may reflect scale/mix; the release does not quantify price, utilization or yield effects. Do not label the entire product contribution structural. R&D and contingency legal fees remain in GAAP operating costs. No company-adjusted EBITDA is substituted and no after-tax net-income measure is used.
