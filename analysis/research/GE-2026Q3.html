Revenue and operating mechanisms

Management attributes CES growth to internal shop visits, heavier work scopes, spares, engine deliveries and price; profit offsets are mix, investment and inflation. This fits a service installed-base mechanism, but pricing and volume contributions are not disclosed separately. CES services RPO was $170,227m versus $154,712m at year-end; total CES RPO $180,428m. This supports visibility, not guaranteed next-quarter revenue. Defense RPO $31,431m versus $25,856m supports programs with execution and funding risks. A $0.1bn tariff-related contract-estimate reversal is disclosed in nine-month discussion; no unsupported allocation of that YTD benefit to Q3 is made. That limits complete quarterly normalization. Corporate savings from functional/EHS costs may be partly recurring; legal/environmental cost timing can reverse.
