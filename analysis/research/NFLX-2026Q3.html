Cash reconciliation and contrary evidence

The exact $337.105m OCF increase reconciles to net income +1,117.011, net content timing −436.609, content amortization +232.710, other D&A +19.774, SBC +52.737, FX remeasurement −7.455, other noncash −22.545, deferred taxes +161.515, other current assets +33.182, payables −93.240, accrued/other liabilities −621.642, deferred revenue −14.137 and other noncurrent items −84.196 (USD millions). The large liability comparison means net-income growth is not cash growth. These are accounting bridge components, not independently proven temporary adjustments. YTD OCF $10,196.296m versus $8,037.631m and FCF $9,502.756m versus $7,588.746m show broader growth, but do not remove content timing. No full TTM cash normalization is asserted.

Base case: recurring subscription economics support profit growth, but elevated Q3 IOMA fades. Adverse case: ad-product delays persist, price increases harm retention, and deferred expense catches up. Ads guidance fell to about $2.7bn from $3bn. Q4 operating income guidance $3,500m versus $2,957m and revenue $13,442m versus $12,051m implies roughly 39.0% IOMA using rounded company forecast/history. Guidance includes about 120bp restructuring impact, approximately $161m of Q4 revenue; excluding it is a separate sensitivity, not a new forecast. Monitor revenue versus guidance, retention, ad rollout, content cash-to-amortization, and real-estate spending. FY2027 depends on reinvestment and ad expansion into 15 countries, which are management plans rather than booked revenue.
